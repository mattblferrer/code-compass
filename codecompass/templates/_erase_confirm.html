Delete all your data?

This deletes everything you have entered or synced:

  • Attempts, with their notes and times
  • Interviews
  • Problems you added yourself
  • Settings, pattern goals, and the site filter
  • Linked usernames, sync items, and rating history

The built-in problem list stays. You can undo this until you save something new, for up to a day.

To keep a copy, download your attempts as CSV first.
