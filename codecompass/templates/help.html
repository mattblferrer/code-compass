Outcomes

Every attempt you log has one of four outcomes. Choose the one that matches the most help you used.

{{ definition }}
{% endfor %}

On a problem from a pattern you have barely tried, you can reveal a beginner hint that names its patterns. A hint counts as help: after you reveal it, your next attempt on that problem cannot be saved as solved alone.

The timer on the log form is optional. Time taken is shown on pattern pages but never affects scores, review dates, or recommendations.

Scores

Each pattern gets a mastery score from 0 to 100: an estimate of how well you can solve its problems without help. A pattern shows "not enough data" until it has {{ min_attempts }} attempts.

  • Each attempt moves the score toward what its outcome is worth. Solving alone pulls it up the most, solving with help less, and not solving pulls it down.
  • A problem harder than the score suggests counts for more when you solve it, and less when you do not.
  • Scores fade without practice: a score halves over {{ fade_days }} days with no attempts.
  • An attempt counts toward every pattern its problem has.
  • Time taken, contest ratings, and target or mastered goals never change a score.

The dashboard shows every pattern's score. Open a pattern to see the attempts behind it, and Progress for scores over time.

Reviews

A problem you attempt comes back for review. The first wait depends on how it went:

{% for key, label, _, days in outcomes %} {% endfor %}
OutcomeDue again after
{{ label }}{{ days }} days
  • Each review solved alone doubles the wait. A review with any other outcome starts the wait again from that outcome's first value.
  • Trying a problem again before it is due starts its wait again, and does not count as a review.
  • When a review is due, you are usually offered a similar problem you have not tried: one that shares a pattern and is about as hard. Solving either one completes the review.
  • "Not now" hides a new problem for {{ not_now_days }} days. On a review's similar problem, it offers another one, and the review stays due.
  • The review of a problem last practiced more than {{ stale_days }} days ago waits while any more recent review is due.

Reviews lists what is due now and soon. To stop reviews, turn off Review problems in settings.

Syncing

Link a public username for {{ sync_names }} in settings, then press Sync now on the Sync page. CodeCompass never asks for another site's password, and contacts a site only when you start a sync.

  • A sync lists your new submissions with no outcome, because an accepted submission does not say whether you had help. Choose an outcome for each, or select several and choose once, to save them as attempts; dismiss the ones you do not want.
  • A sync also reads your contest rating history, shown on Profile. Ratings never affect scores, reviews, or recommendations.

Pattern guides

Each pattern's page lists guides from other sites that teach it, and you can add your own links there. Guides aimed at interviews come first for core patterns, and guides for competitive programming first for advanced ones. A guide marked "trick" teaches a technique from another topic that helps with the pattern. Some sites need a free account.

Reading a guide during an attempt counts as help, so log that attempt as solved with hints. Reading one beforehand, to learn the pattern, does not.
