What is kept

  • From GitHub: your account's ID number, username, and display name, to know it is you. No email, no password, and no access to your repositories.
  • What you enter or sync: attempts, notes, links, interviews, problems you add, settings and goals, linked Codeforces and AtCoder usernames, synced submissions, and rating history.
  • A sign-in record per browser, which ends after {{ session_days }} days without use or when you sign out.

Why

Only to show you your own practice: scores, reviews, and recommendations. Your data is never shown to other accounts, sold, or used for anything else. A sync contacts Codeforces and AtCoder only when you start it, by the public username you link.

Removing it

Settings can download all your data as a backup file, or delete it. Your account page deletes the account and everything in it at once. The database host keeps a short history for recovery, so deleted data is fully gone once that window passes.

This site is a small project for practice and testing, open to at most {{ max_accounts }} accounts.
