    • Mastery by pattern

      Every pattern, from two pointers to dynamic programming, gets a score from 0 to 100 after three attempts. Harder problems count for more, help counts for less, and old results fade, so the score shows what you can do now. Open any pattern to see the attempts behind it.

    • Reviews before you forget

      A problem you solved comes back for review after a while, and sooner if you needed hints or the solution. Each review you pass waits longer for the next. A review usually brings a similar problem you have not tried rather than the same one, so you practice the pattern instead of recalling one answer.
